# 👟 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Pivot Tables, and advanced dashboarding techniques.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Adidas sales performance across different states, regions, retailers, and product categories. It enables users to monitor revenue, profitability, sales trends, and key business metrics through interactive filters and visualizations.

## Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Track profit and profitability trends.
- Identify top-performing products and retailers.
- Compare regional and state-wise sales performance.
- Support data-driven business decision-making.
- Provide interactive reporting using Excel.

## Data Preparation

Data cleaning and transformation were performed using Power Query.

### Steps Performed

1. Imported raw sales dataset.
2. Removed duplicate records.
3. Checked and handled missing values.
4. Corrected data types.
5. Standardized column names.
6. Created calculated fields.
7. Loaded transformed data into Excel Data Model.
8. Built Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts.
9. Designed an interactive dashboard.

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

| KPI | Value |

| Total Units Sold | 6M |

---

### Monthly Sales Trend

Displays monthly sales performance to identify seasonal patterns and sales fluctuations.

**Insights:**
- January recorded the highest sales.
- June recorded the lowest sales.
- Sales recovered strongly from August onwards.

---

### State Wise Sales Analysis

Interactive map visualization showing sales distribution across states.

**Insights:**
- Higher sales contribution is observed from states such as California, Texas, and New York.
- Geographic analysis helps identify strong and weak markets.

---

### Region Wise Sales Analysis

Donut chart comparing sales contribution by region.

**Insights:**
- West Region contributes the highest share of total sales.
- South Region is the second-highest contributor.
- East Region contributes the lowest share.

---

### Product Wise Sales Analysis

Top-performing product categories:

1. Men's Street Footwear
2. Women's Apparel
3. Men's Athletic Footwear
4. Women's Street Footwear
5. Men's Apparel
6. Women's Athletic Footwear

**Insights:**
- Men's Street Footwear generates the highest revenue.
- Footwear categories dominate overall sales.

---

### Retailer Wise Sales Analysis

Top retailers contributing to total sales:

- Foot Locker
- Sports Direct
- West Gear
- Walmart
- Kohl's
- Amazon

**Insights:**
- Foot Locker is the highest revenue-generating retailer.
- Sports Direct closely follows in sales performance.

## Key Business Insights

### Sales Performance
- Total Sales reached $295.59M.
- Total Profit reached $107.99M.
- Profit Margin remained strong at 39.49%.

### Product Performance
- Footwear products contribute the majority of revenue.
- Men's Street Footwear is the best-selling category.

### Retailer Performance
- Foot Locker and Sports Direct are major contributors to overall revenue.

### Regional Performance
- West Region leads total sales contribution.
- Regional analysis helps prioritize market expansion opportunities.

### Monthly Trend Analysis
- Peak sales occurred during January and September.
- Lowest sales occurred in June.
- Seasonal patterns can assist future planning and inventory management.
